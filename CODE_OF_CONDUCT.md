# Code of Conduct

KrosScope adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The Covenant governs; this page summarises it in plain words and says how to report a
problem. The summary is adapted from the Covenant, which is available under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 licence.

## Where it applies

It applies in every space of the project: issues, pull requests, review comments and any other official
channel. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## What we expect

- Be respectful of other people and of views and experience that differ from yours.
- Give feedback that is constructive and specific, and accept it in the same way.
- Own your mistakes, apologise for them and learn from them.
- Put the health of the whole community ahead of winning an argument.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting a problem

Report a violation privately, through the private vulnerability reporting on the repository's Security tab
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Say that the report concerns the Code of Conduct, and describe what
happened and where. Do not open a public issue.

The maintainers review every report, respond to it, and respect the privacy and security of the person who
made it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide how to respond, in proportion to the harm. A response can be a private request to
change behaviour, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. They may also edit
or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code.

If you have a question about this code of conduct, open an issue.
